cdshrink - PC Engine CD games without the music, for AmiPCEngine

cdshrink makes a small copy of a CD game for AmiPCEngine. The CD music is
silent in the copy. Sound effects and ADPCM speech stay, as they are in
the game's data. Speech recorded as a CD audio track goes with the music.

Examples:
    Valis II              486 MB ->   7 MB
    Gate of Thunder       533 MB ->  26 MB
    Rondo of Blood        513 MB ->  41 MB
    Strider Hiryuu        485 MB -> 102 MB
    Kabuki Ittouryoudan   613 MB -> 251 MB  (a lot of data on that disc)


What you need
  * A PC, Mac or Linux machine with Python 3.6 or later (python.org).
    The conversion is done there, not on the Amiga.
  * The game as .cue with .bin or .iso files. For a .chd you also need
    chdman, which comes with MAME.
  * AmiPCEngine 2.5 and the System Card 3.0, as for any CD game.


cdshrink.py and this text are in the "PC Tools" drawer of the AmiPCEngine
archive.


Steps (Windows)
  1. Put cdshrink.py in the folder that holds the game.

  2. Open a command window there: in Explorer, click the address bar,
     type cmd and press Enter.

  3. Only if the game is a .chd, unpack it first:

       chdman extractcd -i "Game.chd" -o "Game.cue" -ob "Game.bin"

     (Give the full path to chdman.exe if Windows can't find it.)

  4. Make the small copy:

       py cdshrink.py "Game.cue"

     It ends with "1 converted, 0 not converted."

  5. Copy BOTH files from the new "No music" folder (Game.cue and
     Game.bin) to one drawer on the Amiga.

  6. In AmiPCEngine press Insert, pick Game.cue and play.

On a Mac or Linux step 4 is:

       python3 cdshrink.py "Game.cue"

To convert many games at once give a folder instead of a .cue. Every game
below it gets its own "No music" folder:

       py cdshrink.py "D:\PC Engine CD"


Good to know
  * Your files are only read. An existing small copy is never overwritten:
    delete it first to make it again.
  * Keep the cue's name. AmiPCEngine names the save file after it, so the
    small copy and the full game then share their saves.
  * Don't rename the .bin on its own: the .cue names it.
  * On an Amiga disk that allows only 30 characters in a name, shorten
    the cue's name BEFORE converting.
  * Don't just cut the data track out by hand. Games look tracks up by
    number and position: Gate of Thunder hangs on such a disc. cdshrink
    keeps every track in its place.
  * The small copy also loads quicker and needs much less Fast RAM.
  * WAV, OGG and MP3 tracks are not supported, as in AmiPCEngine itself.


Known limits
  * Played into stage 1 from small copies on a PiStorm A1200 and in
    WinUAE: Gate of Thunder, Rondo of Blood and Strider Hiryuu (Arcade
    Card). Other games and later stages are untested.
  * The small copies are meant for AmiPCEngine. Other emulators and CD
    writers may not accept them.
