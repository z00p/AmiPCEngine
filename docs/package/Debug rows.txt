AmiPCEngine debug rows (the "Debug" icons)

Two rows of hex numbers at the top of the screen, updated every 50 Amiga
vertical blanks (one second on PAL). Times are in scan lines (CIA-B TOD).
AGA display: a panel above the picture, and the picture moves down; on an
NTSC screen the bottom 15 lines are cut. RTG display: the same rows drawn
1:1 on the top 17 lines of the RTG screen (on 960x540 in the border; on
640x480 over the top 8 lines of a 2x picture).

Is it at full speed? Look at FRMS and VBLS in row 2. If they are equal
(0032 = 50 each) every emulated frame fitted in one Amiga frame. WAIT
is the spare time (a high number means plenty of room), and LATE counts
frames that did not fit.

Row 1:  TOTL WRST OTHR
  TOTL  lines in the window (NTSC 50 x 262 = 332C, PAL 50 x 313 = 3D22)
  WRST  longest single frame (one vertical blank = 0106 NTSC, 0139 PAL)
  OTHR  lines in the rest of the main loop

Row 2:  FRMS VBLS WAIT CPU  BG   SPR  VBLI LATE RTG  COPY
  FRMS  PCE frames emulated (full speed = VBLS, 0032 = 50)
  VBLS  Amiga vertical blanks in the window
  WAIT  lines spent waiting for the next vertical blank (spare time)
  CPU   lines in the HuC6280 frame
  BG    lines drawing the background on AGA (0 on RTG)
  SPR   lines drawing the sprites on AGA (0 on RTG)
  VBLI  lines in the vertical blank interrupt
  LATE  frames that took more than one vertical blank
  RTG   RTG only: lines composing the frame in fast RAM (background, sprites)
  COPY  RTG only: lines copying the changed lines to the graphics card

Photograph the rows with your report: the game, the icon, the machine.
